How this works

01

Dataset

Trained on a student social-media & mental-health survey covering usage habits, sleep, study time, and self-reported stress.

02

Features

12 inputs — demographics, platform choice, daily screen time and unlocks, study/activity/sleep hours, and stress level — plus a grouped-country signal.

03

Model

A regression model maps these habits to a single 0–10 wellness score, served through a FastAPI backend.

04

The score

Higher is better. It's a pattern-based signal from the training data, not a clinical or diagnostic measure.

Built for informational purposes only — this is not a clinical assessment. If you're struggling, please talk to someone you trust.
